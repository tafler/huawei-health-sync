# Privacy Policy for HealthWatchSync

**Last Updated:** March 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HealthWatchSync ("the App") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information. HealthWatchSync is a personal utility application designed to sync health data from Huawei Watch GT 3 Pro to your personal Google Drive account. **The App does not collect, store, or share any personal data with any third-party servers or services beyond your explicit control.**

## 1. Data Collection and Use

HealthWatchSync connects to Huawei Health Kit to access the following health data from your Huawei Watch GT 3 Pro:

*   **Heart Rate:** Average, minimum, and maximum heart rate measurements.
*   **Step Count:** Daily step count.
*   **Sleep Data:** Total sleep duration, deep sleep, and REM sleep duration.
*   **SpO2:** Blood oxygen saturation levels (if supported by your device and Huawei Health Kit access).

This data is collected solely for the purpose of transferring it to your designated Google Drive folder for personal tracking and analysis. The App processes this data directly on your device and does not transmit it to any external servers operated by us or any third parties.

## 2. Data Storage

*   **On Device:** Access and refresh tokens for Huawei Health Kit and Google Drive are stored securely on your device using AndroidX Security Crypto (EncryptedSharedPreferences). No health data is persistently stored on your device by the App itself, beyond temporary processing.
*   **Google Drive:** Your health data is formatted as JSON snapshots and uploaded directly to a specific folder within your personal Google Drive account. You retain full ownership and control over this data in your Google Drive.

## 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

**HealthWatchSync DOES NOT share, sell, rent, or disclose your personal health data to any third parties.** Your data remains exclusively within your control and your personal Google Drive account.

## 4. Third-Party Services

*   **Huawei Health Kit:** The App integrates with Huawei Health Kit to read your health data. Your use of Huawei Health Kit is subject to Huawei's own terms and privacy policies.
*   **Google Drive API:** The App uses the Google Drive API to upload your health data. Your use of Google Drive is subject to Google's own terms and privacy policies.

## 5. Your Rights

As the owner of your data, you have the following rights concerning your health information:

*   **Access:** You can access all your health data directly from your Google Drive account.
*   **Modification:** You can modify or correct your data within your Google Drive.
*   **Deletion:** You can delete your data from your Google Drive at any time. You can also revoke the App's access to Huawei Health Kit and Google Drive through the respective service settings on your device or in your account.

## 6. Security

We are committed to protecting the security of your information. The App uses industry-standard security measures, such as secure token storage (EncryptedSharedPreferences) and HTTPS for API communication, to protect your data during transfer and storage on your device.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
